This page covers the @FDFSAlertsBot Telegram bot. The Chrome extension has its own policy — it has no server and keeps everything in your browser.

What the bot stores

  • Your Telegram chat ID — the number Telegram uses for your chat with the bot, so alerts reach you. Not your phone number, and not your name.
  • Your account — your account code, film credits left, whether your free film is used, and which films (and cities) you have added.
  • Your watches — the films, cities, theatres, languages, formats and shows you picked, your settings such as how many seats together, and what the last checks found.
  • Your payments — for each pack you buy: the Razorpay payment ID, the amount, how many credits it added, and when.
  • Server logs — short technical lines (for example "seat check failed") which may include your chat ID, kept for troubleshooting.

The bot reads only the messages you send to it, in your private chat with it.

Payments

Payments are made on a Razorpay payment page, not in the bot. Razorpay handles your card, UPI or bank details under its own privacy policy; we never see them. From each payment we read only the amount, its status, its payment ID and the account code you typed, to add your credits. Razorpay also shows us the email and phone number you enter on its page, as part of our merchant account; the bot does not copy them.

Who else is involved

  • Telegram carries the messages between you and the bot.
  • District is asked about the films and shows you watch. These requests carry nothing about you — no account, no chat ID — and are the same for everyone watching that film.
  • Razorpay processes payments, as above.
  • Oracle Cloud hosts the server the bot runs on.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, and there is no advertising, analytics or tracking.

How long it is kept

  • A seat watch is deleted a few hours after its show starts; a booking-opens watch after its film is out. Stopping a watch deletes it at once.
  • Your account and payment records are kept while you use the bot, and payment records for as long as Indian tax law requires.

Deleting your data

Email gosulayaswanth2@gmail.com from anywhere with your account code and we will delete your account and watches within 7 days, keeping only the payment records the law requires. Unused credits are lost when an account is deleted.
