2. What the bot does

You share a film from District to the bot in a private Telegram chat. The bot then checks District for you, from a server, so it keeps working while your laptop is shut:

  • Booking opens: it tells you when tickets for the film go on sale at the theatres you picked, including premiere shows.
  • Seats: it tells you when seats you asked for become free at a show you picked.

The bot only watches and alerts. It never books tickets, holds seats or pays for anything on your behalf, and it does not need or ask for your District account. Seat Watch is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to District or BookMyShow.
